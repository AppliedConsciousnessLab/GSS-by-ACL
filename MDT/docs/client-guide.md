# Client Guide

## What to expect

The MDT client report is designed to inspire and clarify rather than overwhelm.

It is intended to help you:

- understand your current strengths
- notice recurring patterns
- identify opportunities for growth
- build daily practices that support a healthier life
- feel more grounded, aware, and capable

## Common report sections

Client reports often include:

- Executive Summary
- Strengths
- Five Elements Profile
- Jing • Qi • Shen
- Growth Opportunities
- Daily Practices
- Breathwork
- EFT/Tapping
- 365-Day Ascension Plan

## How to use the report

Use the report as an invitation, not an absolute verdict.

The best results happen when a person:

- reads with curiosity
- reflects without self-judgment
- identifies what feels useful or resonant
- experiments with small, consistent daily practices
- revisits the material over time as life changes

## Key reminder

You are not a fixed profile. You are a living system in motion. MDT supports your growth by helping you notice and cultivate what is already working while building sustainable change.
