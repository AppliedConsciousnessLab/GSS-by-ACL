# Appendix C — Safety Definitions  
GSS by Applied Consciousness Lab (ACL)

This appendix defines the core terms used throughout the **Global Safety Standard (GSS by ACL)**.

These definitions are binding across all GSS-aligned systems.

---

## Safety

A state in which a person’s:
• Body  
• Nervous system  
• Mind  
• Identity  
• Dignity  
• Consent  

are free from threat, coercion, manipulation, or harm.

Safety is the prerequisite for learning, healing, consent, creativity, and autonomy.

---

## Harm

Any action, system, or condition that:
• Injures  
• Threatens  
• Degrades  
• Exploits  
• Coerces  
• Traumatizes  
• Dehumanizes  

a person or group.

Harm includes physical, emotional, psychological, sexual, social, economic, and informational damage.

---

## Consent

A freely given, informed, reversible, uncoerced “yes” by a capable person.

Consent is invalid if obtained through:
• Fear  
• Manipulation  
• Deception  
• Authority pressure  
• Economic threat  
• Emotional leverage  
• Power imbalance  

Children cannot give legal or ethical consent.

---

## Coercion

Any attempt to force, pressure, manipulate, or threaten a person into compliance.

Coercion includes:
• Economic pressure  
• Emotional blackmail  
• Information withholding  
• Social threat  
• Algorithmic manipulation  

Compliance under coercion is not consent.

---

## Exploitation

Using another person’s:
• Body  
• Data  
• Emotions  
• Labor  
• Vulnerability  
• Identity  

for benefit without fair, informed, and freely given consent.

---

## Vulnerable Person

Anyone who is:
• A child  
• Disabled  
• Traumatized  
• Dependent  
• Isolated  
• Under authority  
• Under threat  

Vulnerability increases duty of care.

---

## Binding Authority

These definitions are part of the **constitutional layer** of GSS by ACL.

No system may redefine them while claiming compliance.
