# Appendix H — Enforcement & Remedies  
GSS by Applied Consciousness Lab (ACL)

This appendix defines how violations of **GSS by ACL** are handled.

Safety without consequences is theater.

---

## 1. Loss of Compliance

Any system that violates:
• Child protection
• Content boundaries
• Consent
• AI safety
• Attribution
• Risk reporting

immediately loses the right to claim **GSS by ACL** compliance.

No warnings are required for critical violations.

---

## 2. Public Revocation

ACL may publicly state when a system is no longer compliant.

This protects:
• Users
• Children
• Researchers
• The integrity of the standard

Transparency is a safety tool.

---

## 3. Restoration Path

A system may regain compliance only after:
• The harm is acknowledged
• The cause is fixed
• Safeguards are added
• The fix is documented publicly

No silent reinstatement.

---

## 4. Why This Exists

Standards without enforcement become marketing slogans.

GSS by ACL exists to protect humans, not brands.
