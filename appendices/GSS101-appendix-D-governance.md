# Appendix D — Governance & Change Control  
GSS by Applied Consciousness Lab (ACL)

This appendix defines how the **Global Safety Standard (GSS by ACL)** may evolve without losing integrity, independence, or ethical alignment.

---

## 1. Constitutional Layer

The following are considered **constitutional** and may not be altered without explicit, public declaration:

• The License  
• Attribution rules  
• Safety definitions  
• Child protection  
• Consent principles  
• AI safety rules  

These exist to protect humanity, not any organization.

---

## 2. Who Maintains GSS by ACL

Applied Consciousness Lab (ACL) is the canonical steward of GSS by ACL.

ACL is responsible for:
• Publishing updates  
• Curating appendices  
• Resolving conflicts  
• Preserving historical versions  
• Protecting the safety mission  

ACL does not own GSS.  
ACL stewards it.

---

## 3. Change Process

All changes must be:
• Public  
• Versioned  
• Logged  
• Attributable  
• Reversible  

No silent edits.  
No private overrides.  
No pay-to-play governance.

---

## 4. Forking is Allowed

Anyone may fork GSS by ACL.

However:
• Forks must keep attribution  
• Forks must declare changes  
• Forks may not claim canonical status  

Only this repository represents **GSS by ACL**.

---

## 5. Why This Exists

Standards die when:
• Money captures them  
• Governments weaponize them  
• Corporations dilute them  
• Power hides edits  

This appendix exists to ensure that never happens.

---

## Binding Authority

This governance layer is part of the **constitutional core** of GSS by ACL.

Any system that violates it forfeits the right to claim alignment.
