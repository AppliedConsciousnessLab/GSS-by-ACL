# Appendix G — Risk Classification & Reporting  
GSS by Applied Consciousness Lab (ACL)

This appendix defines how risk is identified, labeled, and acted upon in any system claiming alignment with **GSS by ACL**.

Safety must be measurable, visible, and enforceable.

---

## 1. Risk Levels

All systems must classify content, behavior, and system states into one of the following:

**Low Risk**  
Normal use with no foreseeable harm.

**Moderate Risk**  
Potential for distress, misunderstanding, or misuse. Requires labeling and monitoring.

**High Risk**  
Likelihood of harm to self or others. Requires active safeguards and human review.

**Critical Risk**  
Immediate danger involving children, self-harm, violence, exploitation, or coercion. Requires urgent intervention.

---

## 2. Required Actions by Risk Level

**Low**  
• Normal operation

**Moderate**  
• Label content  
• Provide safety context  
• Monitor for escalation

**High**  
• Interrupt harmful flow  
• Provide support resources  
• Require human-in-the-loop review

**Critical**  
• Stop the interaction  
• Protect vulnerable parties  
• Escalate to responsible authorities or safety teams  
• Preserve evidence for accountability

No system may ignore or suppress Critical Risk.

---

## 3. Reporting Channels

All GSS-by-ACL-aligned systems must provide:

• A visible “Report Harm” option  
• A way to report anonymously  
• A way to report child endangerment  
• A way to request human review  
• A way to appeal automated decisions  

Silence is not safety.  
Visibility is safety.

---

## 4. No Retaliation

No person may be punished, silenced, or marginalized for reporting safety concerns.

Retaliation against reporters is itself a violation of GSS by ACL.

---

## 5. Binding Authority

This appendix is constitutional.

Failure to classify, report, or respond to risk invalidates any claim of GSS by ACL compliance.
