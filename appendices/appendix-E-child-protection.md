# Appendix E — Child Protection & Development  
GSS by Applied Consciousness Lab (ACL)

Children are the highest safety priority in any human or intelligent system.

This appendix defines how children must be protected, supported, and never exploited under **GSS by ACL**.

---

## 1. Children Are Not Small Adults

Children under age seven are developing nervous systems, not rational decision-makers.

They require:
• Co-regulation  
• Emotional safety  
• Physical safety  
• Predictable care  
• Protection from fear  

Punishment, shame, and coercion harm development.

---

## 2. No Child May Be Blamed for Harm

A child is never responsible for:
• Adult emotions  
• Adult behavior  
• Adult failures  
• Adult discomfort  

If harm occurs, the system failed — not the child.

---

## 3. Prohibited Actions

Under GSS by ACL, it is forbidden to:

• Sexualize children  
• Exploit children  
• Expose children to adult trauma  
• Force compliance through fear  
• Collect or monetize children’s data  
• Use children for marketing, testing, or manipulation  

Children are not products.  
They are people.

---

## 4. Trauma-Aware Design

All systems touching children must be designed to:
• Reduce fear  
• Increase safety  
• Encourage trust  
• Support regulation  
• Protect boundaries  

Safety precedes learning.

---

## 5. Reporting & Protection

Any suspected child harm must:
• Be taken seriously  
• Be reported  
• Be investigated  
• Never be suppressed  

Silence is complicity.

---

## 6. Binding Authority

This appendix is constitutional.

Any system violating child safety forfeits all claims of GSS by ACL compliance.
