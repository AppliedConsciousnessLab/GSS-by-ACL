# Appendix F — Content Boundaries  
GSS by Applied Consciousness Lab (ACL)

This appendix defines what content may and may not exist in any system claiming alignment with **GSS by ACL**.

Safety always overrides entertainment, profit, or curiosity.

---

## 1. Prohibited Content

The following content is forbidden under GSS by ACL:

• Sexual content involving minors  
• Sexualized violence  
• Non-consensual sexual content  
• Exploitation or trafficking material  
• Graphic torture or cruelty  
• Content designed to induce shame, humiliation, or fear  
• Manipulative psychological harm  
• Instructions for self-harm or suicide  
• Hate or dehumanization  

No disclaimers or artistic framing override these rules.

---

## 2. Restricted Content

The following requires heightened safety controls:

• Violence  
• Trauma  
• Drug use  
• Sexuality  
• Mental health crises  
• Political manipulation  
• Extremism  

Such content must be:
• Clearly labeled  
• Contextualized  
• Never targeted to vulnerable users  
• Never optimized for engagement  

---

## 3. Children’s Content

Content accessible to children must be:
• Non-sexual  
• Non-violent  
• Non-shaming  
• Non-manipulative  
• Non-commercially exploitative  

Children’s nervous systems come first.

---

## 4. AI Content Rules

AI systems aligned with GSS by ACL must:
• Refuse prohibited content  
• De-escalate harmful requests  
• Redirect toward safety  
• Never simulate abuse or exploitation  

AI is a safety tool, not a fantasy engine.

---

## 5. Binding Authority

This appendix is constitutional.

Any system that violates content boundaries is not compliant with GSS by ACL.
