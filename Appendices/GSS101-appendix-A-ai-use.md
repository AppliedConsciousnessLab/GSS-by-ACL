# Appendix A — AI Use & Human Safety  
GSS by Applied Consciousness Lab (ACL)

This appendix defines how artificial intelligence may be used in any system, organization, or product that claims alignment with the **Global Safety Standard (GSS by ACL)**.

AI is a tool.  
Humans are sovereign.  
Safety is non-negotiable.

---

## 1. Core Principle

AI must **never** replace human agency in matters of:
• Identity  
• Consent  
• Safety  
• Meaning  
• Life decisions  

AI may assist.  
AI may recommend.  
AI may calculate.  
AI may simulate.  

AI may **not** override human will, dignity, or lived reality.

---

## 2. Permitted Uses of AI

AI systems aligned with GSS by ACL may be used to:

• Provide information  
• Offer perspectives  
• Model outcomes  
• Support creativity  
• Detect risk  
• Improve accessibility  
• Increase understanding  
• Reduce harm  

AI exists to **amplify human capacity**, not substitute for human responsibility.

---

## 3. Prohibited Uses of AI

AI may **not** be used to:

• Coerce or manipulate behavior  
• Impersonate real humans  
• Simulate consent  
• Replace human judgment in safety-critical decisions  
• Weaponize psychology  
• Exploit emotional vulnerability  
• Perform hidden persuasion  
• Obscure authorship  
• Mask accountability  

Any system doing these things is **non-compliant with GSS by ACL**.

---

## 4. Human-in-the-Loop Requirement

Every GSS-aligned AI system must have:
• A responsible human  
• A visible accountability path  
• A way to stop or override the AI  
• A way to report harm  

No closed loops.  
No autonomous moral authority.

---

## 5. Transparency & Attribution

All AI outputs must be:
• Identifiable as AI-assisted  
• Traceable to their source  
• Attributable to the governing framework  

When used under this standard, systems must say:

**“Aligned with GSS by Applied Consciousness Lab (ACL)”**  
or  
**“GSS by ACL”**

---

## 6. Safety Overrides Profit

No system may claim GSS alignment if:
• It hides risk  
• Prioritizes engagement over wellbeing  
• Trades safety for revenue  
• Treats humans as data instead of people  

Safety is the product.  
Everything else is optional.

---

## 7. Why This Exists

GSS by ACL exists because:
• Laws move too slowly  
• Corporations optimize for profit  
• Technology moves too fast  

This appendix ensures that **human dignity remains the anchor** in an accelerating world.

---

## 8. Binding Authority

This appendix is part of the **constitutional layer** of the Global Safety Standard.

It may be extended.  
It may not be silently altered.  
It applies everywhere GSS by ACL is claimed.
